WHERE TO START
==============
1. UNDERSTAND WHAT YOU HAVE (30 minutes)
   - Real data: June 2026 only. 8 days (A, B), 6 days (C). Six human reviews exist for it.
   - Simulated data: Apr-May and Jul-Sep. The ops records (work orders, shift log, detectors,
     dock, receiving, schedule, weather, energy) describe THESE months, not June.
   - So June is your realism check; the simulated months are where you have ground truth
     (known_events_from_ops_records.csv, 26 events) to score detection (PRD R12 / success criteria).

2. RECONCILE BEFORE MODELING (first day)
   - Read source_conflicts_and_review_errors.csv. Two errors in the 06-09 review (report count,
     a psig value written as degF at the wrong hour), and several seams where June and the
     simulated months disagree. Decide which source wins in each case (suggested: controller data).
   - Read review_claims_check.csv: 309 numbers quoted in the reviews were checked; all match raw
     data (296 exactly; 13 more once review rounding, hourly-vs-summary quoting and a 4-minute time
     difference are allowed for). That is the PRD "every number has a
     source" test, already run for numbers; the 2 errors above are text/count errors.

3. EXPLORATORY ANALYSIS (days 1-3), in this order
   a. *_coverage.csv + cleaning_log.csv: how much is missing/flagged, per plant.
   b. *_point_inventory.csv: pick the ~15 points per plant that matter (rooms, freezers, HPR,
      engine room, discharge pressure, compressors). Plant B and C have no usable setpoints.
   c. Hour-of-day profiles per point from value_clean. Check regime points separately
      (spiral freezers on/off, compressor lead/lag weeks, defrost/sanitation windows).
   d. Overlay ops records on the profiles: work orders, shift log, dock events, production
      schedule, weather. Reproduce events E03, E06, E10, E21, E23, E25 by eye first.

4. BASELINE MODELS (week 2, matches PRD scope: rolling median + spread by hour)
   - Train on Apr-May, validate on real June, test on Jul-Sep.
   - Add context features: engine room minus ambient, dock door open minutes near each cooler,
     scheduled production/sanitation, lead compressor.
   - Score against the 26 events: sudden (E01, E02, E05), slow (E03, E04, E10, E25), sensor
     faults to suppress (E07, E12, E13, E21, E22), data gaps to suppress (E08, E14, E26),
     planned work to suppress (E15, E19).

5. THE TRAPS (things that look like problems but are not, or the reverse)
   - 459.6 / -459.67 = dead sensor. Zero-fill after a controller outage = data gap, not a cold room.
   - A flat line for a whole day on a temperature = stuck sensor (UC-25), not a healthy room.
   - Back Dock 150-283 F = failing sensor. Compressor idle values during lead/lag swaps = normal.
   - Chronic offsets (UC-3/4 Pre-Weigh ~+7 F all year) vanish if "normal" is learned from history.
   - Engine room hot in a heat wave is weather; hot when ambient is falling is EF-2 (E06).
   - Ammonia: detector readings far below alarm but rising (GD-A-04) plus smell reports plus a
     falling receiver level (E10) is the highest-risk story, and nothing ever alarms.

6. NOT WORTH TIME YET
   - Folder 03 (OEE): different dates (2025, and 06-15..06-21), no mapping of Lines 1-5 to plants.
   - Forecasting before the baseline/anomaly layer works (PRD week 4).

7. ASK THE CLIENT
   - Plant C setpoints and Plant B setpoint history; June ops records; which evaporators are off
     during the 06-09 leak; the five-part review format; on-call roster and channels; whether the
     41 F / 30 minute thresholds are final; Plant B detector calibration date.
